# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, customer-first project management framework designed to deliver value iteratively while maintaining clear ownership, data-driven decisions, and psychological safety. This documentation suite guides teams through each phase of the project lifecycle—from initial problem validation through post-project learning.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Project Lifecycle Overview

OctoAcme projects follow five key phases:

### 1. **Initiation** — Validate & Authorize
Confirm business need, identify stakeholders, and decide go/no-go for planning.
- **Key deliverable**: Project One-pager with problem statement, goals, and success metrics
- **Gate**: Stakeholder alignment and sponsor approval

### 2. **Planning** — Design & Scope
Turn an approved initiative into an actionable plan and prioritized backlog.
- **Key deliverable**: Prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, release timeline, and risk register
- **Gate**: Clear Definition of Done and confirmed team availability

### 3. **Execution** — Build & Track
Manage day-to-day delivery, quality assurance, and progress tracking.
- **Key deliverable**: Shippable increments, passing CI/QA, regular demos
- **Gate**: Acceptance criteria met and quality standards achieved

### 4. **Release** — Deploy & Verify
Standardize how features reach production to reduce risk and improve observability.
- **Key deliverable**: Release notes, rollback plan, post-deploy verifications
- **Gate**: All pre-release requirements met

### 5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** — Learn & Iterate
Capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements.
- **Key deliverable**: Retrospective notes and action items for future projects
- **Gate**: Continuous improvement culture embedded in team rhythm

## How to Use These Docs

### For New Team Members
1. Start with the **Project Management Overview** to understand OctoAcme's principles and key roles
2. Read the phase guides relevant to your role and current project stage
3. Reference role-specific guidance in **Roles & Personas**
4. Keep the **Risk Management & Communication** guide handy for cross-cutting concerns

### For Project Managers
- Use **Project Initiation** to validate and authorize new work
- Follow **Project Planning** to create actionable backlogs and release plans
- Refer to **Execution & Tracking** for team cadence and quality checkpoints
- Leverage **Risk Management & Communication** for escalation and stakeholder updates
- Follow **Release & Deployment** for go-live procedures
- Run **Retrospectives** to drive continuous improvement

### For Product Managers
- Use **Project Initiation** to write compelling Problem Statements and Success Metrics
- Collaborate using templates in **Project Planning** for backlog prioritization
- Reference **Execution & Tracking** for demo planning and velocity tracking
- Use **Risk Management & Communication** to identify product-level dependencies and stakeholder concerns

### For Developers
- Review **Project Planning** to understand Definition of Done and acceptance criteria
- Follow **Execution & Tracking** for PR workflow, testing, and quality standards
- Use **Release & Deployment** to understand deployment procedures and rollback scenarios
- Participate in **Retrospectives** to share technical learnings
