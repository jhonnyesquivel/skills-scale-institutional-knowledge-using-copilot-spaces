# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and manage the testing strategy across the project lifecycle. They partner with developers and product managers to confirm that work is testable, reliable, and ready for release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated tests across unit, integration, and end-to-end layers
- Validate features against quality standards before release
- Track defects, quality metrics, and release readiness
- Support Definition of Done by confirming that required validation is complete
- Coordinate smoke testing and regression checks for important releases

### Goals
- Reduce defects before production
- Improve confidence in release quality
- Align testing efforts with project priorities and business outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Defect triage and quality reviews
- Release readiness checkpoints and smoke test results
- Collaboration with developers, PMs, and release managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to confirm behavior, test coverage, and fix validation
- Provides Product Managers with quality signals and release confidence
- Supports Project Managers by surfacing timeline risks related to quality or validation gaps

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, prioritization input, and approval authority. They help ensure work stays aligned to strategic goals, resourcing needs, and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm project value and business priority
- Provide success metrics, budget context, and scope direction
- Review milestone progress and approve major decisions or changes
- Remove blockers that require executive or cross-functional support
- Align project trade-offs with organizational goals

### Goals
- Ensure work delivers business value and strategic alignment
- Support decision-making with clear priorities and funding guidance
- Maintain visibility into project outcomes and risk exposure

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and decision gates
- Monthly or quarterly stakeholder updates
- Escalations for business-impacting issues or scope changes
- Release communications and go/no-go approvals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Gives Product Managers direction on priority, outcomes, and business goals
- Provides Project Managers with executive support, approvals, and escalation paths
- Collaborates with Developers and QA leads through outcomes and acceptance feedback rather than day-to-day delivery decisions

---

## Scrum Master / Team Facilitator

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Team Facilitators help keep project work moving smoothly by improving team collaboration, ceremony quality, and delivery flow. They support healthy team practices and remove obstacles that slow work down.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Help the team improve process, communication, and decision-making
- Identify and remove delivery blockers or process friction
- Protect focus time and help maintain sustainable work practices
- Support alignment between team habits and project goals

### Goals
- Improve team rhythm and delivery predictability
- Reduce waste, confusion, and blockages in execution
- Promote learning and continuous team improvement

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and team syncs
- Team health discussions and retrospectives
- Coordination with Project Managers on workflow issues and dependencies
- Supportive communication with Product and Engineering leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on cadence, communication, and dependencies
- Helps Developers maintain clear execution flow without direct task assignment
- Supports Product Managers by improving backlog readiness and team alignment

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects provide technical direction, reduce design ambiguity, and help ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and consistent with standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide solution design and technical trade-offs
- Define architectural standards, patterns, and technical constraints
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration challenges
- Support estimation and sequencing for complex work
- Review major technical decisions with Product and Project leads

### Goals
- Maintain a sound technical foundation for the product
- Minimize rework caused by poor design decisions
- Improve long-term maintainability and integration stability

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical decision discussions
- Architecture walkthroughs and dependency planning
- Risk reviews and technical escalation discussions
- Collaboration during planning and release preparation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers to guide implementation decisions and technical quality
- Works with Product Managers on feasibility, scope, and trade-offs
- Advises Project Managers on technical sequencing, dependencies, and delivery risk

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities and release readiness so changes are introduced with clear communication, checks, and rollback plans.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release windows, timelines, and stakeholder notifications
- Confirm pre-deployment readiness checks are complete
- Manage deployment sequencing and communication during production releases
- Support rollback planning and incident response coordination when needed
- Ensure release notes and operational follow-up are completed

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and disruption
- Improve release predictability and operational readiness
- Support smooth handoff from delivery to production operations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment checklists
- Status updates to project and stakeholder groups
- Post-release verification and issue communications
- Coordination with QA, Security, and Project leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with QA leads to confirm validation and smoke-test readiness
- Coordinates with Project Managers on deployment timing and stakeholder communication
- Supports Developers by aligning release checkpoints with completed work

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads support secure delivery by ensuring risk, compliance, and security requirements are integrated into planning, implementation, and release activities.

### Responsibilities
- Review security requirements and risk exposure for the project
- Support secure design and code review practices
- Coordinate security scanning, remediation, and compliance checks
- Participate in incident response for security-related issues
- Help define security milestones and escalation paths

### Goals
- Reduce security risk and improve compliance readiness
- Prevent late-stage vulnerabilities and release blockers
- Support confidence in secure, resilient delivery

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and risk triage sessions
- Compliance or vulnerability status reporting
- Incident response updates and mitigation coordination
- Collaboration with Product, Project, and Release leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Developers with security guidance and review expectations
- Supports QA by validating that security checks are part of release readiness
- Advises Project Managers and Stakeholders on risk, impacts, and escalation needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles clarify accountability, improve communication, and support better project outcomes across planning, execution, and release.
